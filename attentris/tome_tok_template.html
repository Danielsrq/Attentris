No model here: this is only the rule part of a possible ToMe v3. Sky: in each column, the empty cells from the lower of its two neighbours' tops upwards (a wall counts as full), one token per column; so a well or a gap below both neighbours stays as cells. Filled cells are split into horizontal runs per row, and identical runs in consecutive rows stack into a rectangle; rectangles of at least 2 × 2 become one block token. Every other cell below the surface (holes, wells, slots, ragged surface cells, small filled pieces) stays a single cell, left for merging. Dashed outline: the teacher's move.
