Each cell: the selected layer's lift for that piece token = mean attention per fit_X token of its own piece ÷ mean attention per fit_X token of another piece, over the held-out positions holding both (1 = no preference). "mean" is the 4 heads' attention summed before the ratio. Click a cell to show that layer and head on the board below. The best head is picked after looking, so read its value as an upper end; the mean has no selection.

Reading it. Pick a query: click a piece in the queue or any token row. In "where the query looks", the bars are that token's attention weights over all tokens in the chosen layer and head (they sum to 100%, the query itself included); the board paints each board token's weight through its mask. Per-cell density divides a token's weight by its cell count, so a 4-cell slot and the 100-cell sky compare fairly; whole-token share paints every cell with the full weight. Overlapping overlays add up. Green rows: fit_X / setup_X tokens of the query's own piece (for a T, also the T structures). "Who looks at the query" shows, for each token, how much of its attention goes to the query (rows do not sum to 100%). Piece tokens have no board position: they appear in the list and the queue, not on the board. Attention shows where information is read from, not proof that it decided the move.
