AtTenTris tokenizer · v1 on bot and made-up boards

Base tokens tile the board exactly once: sky (rows above the highest filled cell), clean wells (runs of one-hole rows sharing a column; length 1 = cheese), one token per play-area row, and bands where the 12-token budget merged the lowest rows. Filled cells are drawn as dark blocks framed in their token's colour. Overlays may overlap anything; at most 8 are kept (T structures first, then 3×4/4×3 pits, then pits by area).
