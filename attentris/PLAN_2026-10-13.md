## Background: the bug
After the first (checkerboard) merge, the A/B split alternated in token order, which is row-major: ~78%
of side-by-side neighbour pairs were eligible to merge vs ~51% of stacked ones (`tome_orient.py`). Fix:
`--tome-split hash` (each blob's side = a hashed bit of board, step, stream and its first cell): 49.4-50.5%
for both directions at every step; deterministic, batch-independent, CUDA = CPU
(`test_tome_split.py`, run first by `tome_v42.sh`). Old checkpoints keep the token split, bit-identical.

## Greedy matching (implemented 2026-10-13, commit 53f68c5; inference-only look done, not trained)
Viewer: https://claude.ai/artifact/JhXrKYJPq1QUkmQuBWLQPn (v4.1 as trained / hash / greedy, merge steps with A/B
sides and blocked pairs). v4.1 at inference, 2000 val positions (data/tome_v42/v41_orient_own_hash_greedy.txt):
top-1 0.615 / 0.614 / 0.617; TSD stem 36% / 44% / 37%; well column 77% / 85% / 86%; pit1 64% / 75% / 74%;
TSD row of 3 85% / 77% / 82%. Next: a 4-layer `--tome-split greedy` model next to the 4-layer hash one.
Drop the A/B split. Per merge step and stream, score every adjacent same-stream pair by key cosine,
then take pairs best-first, skipping any pair whose blob is already used (a greedy matching), until the
step's budget r. Computed in parallel by "locally dominant" rounds: an edge is taken when it is the best
remaining edge of both its blobs; repeat on the free blobs. That gives exactly the sequential greedy
matching; keep its r best edges. Merge mechanics unchanged (size-weighted mean, mask union, proportional
attention). Flag: `--tome-split greedy`.
Tests: equals a sequential reference; no blob twice; only adjacent same-stream pairs; deterministic and
batch-independent; CUDA = CPU; budget shortfall; ms per forward vs hash.
Run: a 4-layer model with v4.2-L4's recipe, compared on IoU / orientation / play.
