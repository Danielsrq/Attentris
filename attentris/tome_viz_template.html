Where the selected move looked: final tokens, mean of 4 heads

No rule tokenizer: the board enters as 200 cell tokens plus 7 piece tokens. After attention in layers 2, 3 and 4, the most similar 4-adjacent blobs merge (45% of the cell tokens each time), so every blob is a connected region; candidates then cross-attend to the final blobs. Layer views colour each blob (darker = filled cells, lighter = empty cells) with its outline. Attention paints the selected move's cross-attention through the final blobs, divided by blob size so a big sky blob and a 3-cell gap compare fairly. Dashed outline: the teacher's move; blue: the selected move (the model's top pick by default). Keys: ← →.
