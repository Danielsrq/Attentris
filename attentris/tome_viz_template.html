Where the selected move looked: final tokens, mean of 4 heads

No rule tokenizer: the board enters as one sky token, solid blocks and single cells, plus 7 piece tokens. After attention in each layer, the most similar 4-adjacent blobs merge (how many by the merge schedule: a fixed share per layer, or — ToMe v4.1 — a per-position budget of 20 + (start − 36)/4 blobs per stream, reached after a gentle 10% first merge), so every blob is a connected region; candidates then cross-attend to the final blobs. The model was trained with one schedule; the others are the same weights merging harder at inference (no retraining). Positions tagged game at merge … come from games played at that schedule, labelled afterwards by the teacher. Layer views colour each blob (darker = filled cells, lighter = empty cells) with its outline. Attention paints the selected move's cross-attention through the final blobs, divided by blob size so a big sky blob and a 3-cell gap compare fairly. Dashed outline: the teacher's move; blue: the selected move (the model's top pick by default). Hover a row of the token list to outline that blob in pink. Keys: ← →.

Merge steps show the blobs going into one merge step (one stream). With an A/B split every blob carries its side for that step (A / B on each cell; only A–B neighbours may merge). Green links: the merges made — already the step's top r, so every one happens (the blobs are drawn before the step; the joined result is the matching after layer view). Pink dashed links: blocked pairs — neighbours on the same side whose keys are more similar than the weakest merge this step made, so they would have merged without the sides. The greedy matching has no sides: it takes the most similar neighbour pairs first, each blob at most once per step.
